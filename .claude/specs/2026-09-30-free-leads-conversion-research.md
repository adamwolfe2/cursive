# Free-leads ladder: conversion research (2026-09-30, Sonnet research agent, directional evidence)

- Benchmarks: Apollo $49-99/user/mo; Clay Launch $185/mo; Seamless ~$147/mo; Cursive existing $97/$197/$247. LinkedIn DFY agencies $6k-12k/mo typical (range $397-25k); pay-per-meeting $50-800.
- Conversion: opt-in (no card) trials ~9%, freemium 1-5%, reverse trial 4-12%. Expect 3-9% here. Usage limit (lead 26) beats time limit. Paywall right after the aha.
- Recommendation: Rung 1 weekly leads self-serve checkout, test $147/mo (25/wk) vs $197; $297 for 100/wk. Rung 2 LinkedIn DFY call-led, from $1,500/mo + $500 setup, weekly leads bundled, optional per-meeting kicker. Rung 3 AI dashboard: call only, 14-day build proof point.
- Rung headlines: (1) "Get 25 new leads like these, every Monday." (2) "We message your leads on LinkedIn so you only take the replies." (3) "One dashboard that shows where every lead, reply, and dollar stands."
- Follow-ups after the free 25: in-app at lead 26; +2h "how many can you contact this week?"; day 2 sample opener for one lead; day 5 LinkedIn "swap any that missed"; day 9 read-only-on-day-14 deadline + rung 2.
- Risk: bounce rate is the real product. Never overclaim "verified".
